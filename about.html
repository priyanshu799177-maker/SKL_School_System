About S.K.L Public School

Providing quality education since 2000 with discipline, knowledge, values and modern learning.

Our History

S.K.L Public School was established in the year 2000 with the vision of providing quality education to every child. From Nursery onwards, the school focuses on academic excellence, discipline, moral values and personality development.

Vision

To develop responsible, confident and successful students.

Mission

To provide modern education with strong moral values and discipline.

Goal

To prepare students for higher education and a bright future.
